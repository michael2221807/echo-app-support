HUSH: Little Worlds is a children's paper-sticker and drawing playroom. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking or app-operated servers.

Children's information

The app does not ask for a child's name, age, email, location or other personal information. It does not send pictures, drawing, preferences or device identifiers to the developer. Creating does not require camera, microphone, location or photo-library access.

Local artwork and settings

Sticker placement, drawing, picture previews, selected music, story language and other preferences are stored in the app's local container on your device. There is no app-operated cloud synchronization or public gallery. Device backup is controlled by Apple and your system settings; we do not keep copies of children's artwork on our systems.

Stories and sound

Illustrations, short stories, music and sound effects are included with the app. Narration uses voices installed on your device. The app does not record a child's voice or use a developer-operated speech service.

Parent-chosen copying and sharing

Copying and sharing sit behind a parental gate. A grown-up may copy a full static picture to the system clipboard or choose a destination from the native sharing sheet. No picture is shared automatically. The destination you choose handles the shared picture under its own terms and privacy policy. Exported PNGs do not contain game controls, music or animation.

Payment

This release is a paid app download with all current world books included. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or advertisements. Apple processes the app purchase through the App Store. The game does not receive card or bank-account details.

Support and external pages

External privacy and support links are available to grown-ups behind the parent key. If you choose to email support, your email provider handles the message and the support recipient receives the information you intentionally send. Please avoid children's names or private information. This static support website has no advertising or analytics scripts; its hosting provider may process ordinary request information according to its own policy.

Keeping and removing artwork

Your local pictures remain between sessions. Removing the app removes the app's local artwork; export important pictures before removing it. Operating-system backups are handled by Apple and your settings.

Changes

If an update changes data handling, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated for that version. The current app does not collect data for the developer.
