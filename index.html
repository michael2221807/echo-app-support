Help & Support

Start scanning

Open the source menu and choose Demo or Live LiDAR, then tap Start Scanning. Switch between Sonar and Point Cloud while scanning. Live capture requires a LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad; the demo works without LiDAR or camera access.

Read height in Sonar

Colors and shapes distinguish surfaces below, near and above the height of your phone, aligned with gravity. “Level” means within 30 cm of phone height. Brightness also varies with distance. Missing depth is unknown, not proof of empty space.

Pause, save and revisit

Tap Pause Scan, then the bookmark to save a point-cloud snapshot. Open Scan History using the stack icon. Touch and hold a scan to delete it. The app keeps the latest 20 records on this device.

Range, angle and reset

Display range filters visible depth while preserving the captured scene's point-cloud scale. Angle and height controls filter the view; saved snapshots retain the full field within the selected range. After rotating a point cloud, Reset returns to the initial direction and framing.

Pro and restoration

Sonar Pro is a one-time, non-consumable parameter upgrade for this app. It unlocks angle, display-range and height-layer controls. Free scanning and scan history stay available. Use the original Apple purchase account and tap Restore Purchases in the upgrade page. Apple displays your storefront's purchase price.

Low-light Depth

This separate experimental mode shows current depth frames without room mapping. Results depend on the device, lighting and surfaces. It does not guarantee accuracy in total darkness. Avoid torch or screen light when testing darkness.
