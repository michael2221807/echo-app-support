PRIVACY · VERSION 1.0 FIRST RELEASE

Your room stays
on your device.

HUSH is a LiDAR creature-hunting game. This first release describes the current ad-free app. It has no account system, advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK or app-operated server.

Camera and LiDAR

HUSH requests camera access so ARKit can track your iPhone and provide LiDAR depth for real-time gameplay. Camera images are processed on your device. HUSH does not save or upload RGB camera images or continuously scan your room in the background.

Your local collection

Creature seeds, capture records, preferences and game timers are stored locally. A capture can save a point-cloud snapshot of the visible scene, including room surfaces and the virtual creature, for replay in your field notebook. These snapshots are private on-device files; HUSH does not transmit them to us.

Sharing and clipboard

You may choose to copy or share an illustrated creature keepsake. The keepsake contains the creature's portrait and collection details, not the room point cloud. The destination you select handles the shared image under its own policies. Copying writes that image to your device clipboard.

Local notifications

If you enable visitor reminders, iOS schedules local game notifications. These follow the game's visitor timer. They do not involve remote push messages or detecting your room while the app is closed. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings.

Data collection and tracking

The app does not transmit your scans or collection to the developer, use cross-app tracking, or contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Optional contact email is handled by your chosen email provider; include only information you intend to send when asking for support.

Keeping and removing data

Your collection remains on your device between sessions. Removing the app removes its local app data. Device backup and operating-system privacy settings are controlled by Apple. We do not retain copies of your room scans on our systems.

Future updates

If a later version introduces advertising or changes data handling, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated for that version. The current release contains no advertising or in-app purchases.
