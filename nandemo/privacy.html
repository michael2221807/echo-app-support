Your street stays
with you.

Nandemo Jihanki has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking or app-operated servers. The game itself does not go online.

What the game keeps

Your street, purchases owned and settings are stored in the app's container on your device. The widgets read a copy of the street's plan from the same device. Nothing is sent to the developer. When the device is signed in to iCloud, the game's progress is also kept in your own iCloud so your other devices can continue it; the developer cannot read it, and you can turn iCloud for the app off in the system settings. Device backup is handled by Apple and your system settings.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and scheduled on the device when you leave the game. The game asks first, and the system asks again; you can turn them off at any time in Settings. There are no remote push notifications and no reminders to come back.

Purchases

Optional items are sold as in-app purchases and processed by Apple through the App Store. The game only learns which items your account owns; it never receives card or bank details.

Support email

If you email support, your email provider handles the message and the support recipient receives only what you choose to send. This static support website has no advertising or analytics scripts; its hosting provider may process ordinary request information under its own policy.

Children

The game does not ask for a name, age, email, location or any other personal information.

Changes

If an update changes how data is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated for that version.
