Privacy Policy

On-device depth and motion

LiDAR Sonar: ECHO processes depth and device motion on your device. There is no app account and no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. The app does not upload point clouds, RGB camera images or motion data to developer or third-party servers. RGB camera images are not saved.

Permissions

Live scanning uses the rear depth camera. Experimental Low-light Depth also uses device gravity to align height. Device uptime is used to align depth and motion events within the app. The built-in demo remains available without these permissions.

Local records and preferences

A point-cloud snapshot is written locally only when you choose to save it. The app keeps the latest 20 records, and you can delete them in Scan History. App language is stored as a local preference. iOS backups may include local records and preferences according to your backup settings; the app does not upload them itself.

Apple purchases

Apple StoreKit handles Pro purchases, restoration and refunds. The app verifies Apple-provided entitlements on your device and does not receive bank-card details or payment passwords. Apple processes store transactions under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can revoke camera or motion permissions in Settings. Deleting scans does not affect Pro ownership, and restoring purchases does not recover deleted scans.

This website

The public support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process standard technical access logs needed to serve the website. This is separate from the app's on-device processing.
